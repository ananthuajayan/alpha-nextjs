Where we fit

Between software you have to run and OCR you have to check.

Most bureaux we speak to are stuck with two options. Enterprise document software is powerful, but you license it, staff it and run it yourself, and the price is rarely on the page. Cloud OCR is cheap per page, but it hands back raw text, with no review step, no accuracy promise and nothing in your client's format.

We sit in between. Extraction as a managed service: priced per page, measured on your own documents before you sign anything, delivered in your exact import format, with people checking whatever the AI isn't sure of.

Your team stops keying. Your client sees the same output, sooner.

Two ways to buy

Pay per page, or own the system.

Option 1

Per-page service

You send scans to us over SFTP. We run extraction on our platform and send back checked data in your import format. There's no software to buy and nothing to install.

  • Charged per page processed
  • Our review team handles uncertain fields, or yours does
  • Volumes can go up and down with your contracts

Option 2

Your own system

We set up a pipeline you run yourselves, either by configuring our existing platform for you or by building something bespoke around your process.

  • You own the system and its documentation
  • Runs on your infrastructure or a UK/EU cloud of your choice
  • We train your team and support it month to month

Every quote is based on your volumes and document types, after we've tested a sample batch.

Governance
