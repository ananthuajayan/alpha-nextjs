BoxScan

Audit the archive before you scan it.

BoxScan tells you what must be digitised, what must be kept, and what can be securely destroyed, so you only pay to scan what your retention rules say has to live.

Talk to us

Who it's for

Anyone holding a large legacy archive.

Records bureaux, storage operators and organisations with rooms full of boxes. BoxScan is Phase 0 of any digitisation programme: the step that stops you scanning paper you were allowed to destroy.

How it works

From box to decision.

  1. Capture

    A purpose-built workflow photographs each box, with camera, barcode and labelling built in.

  2. Catalogue

    Box contents and dates are recorded and barcoded.

  3. Apply retention rules

    Your retention schedule is applied box by box.

  4. Decide

    Each box is marked to digitise, retain, or securely destroy, with the reasoning recorded.

Governance

Every decision on record.

  • An audit trail for each box decision
  • Retention rules you set and can review
  • UK or EU hosting agreed per engagement
