How it works

Four steps, with people where it matters.

  1. Ingest

    Scanned documents arrive by secure transfer from your capture process.

  2. Extract

    AI pulls the required fields, including handwriting, and scores its confidence in each.

  3. Review

    Low-confidence items go to a curation portal where your staff confirm or correct them.

  4. Export

    Results are returned in your existing import format, so nothing downstream changes.

Governance & data residency

Your data stays where you agree.

  • UK-resident cloud infrastructure (London region), or EU (Dublin) for Irish clients
  • Encrypted transfer over SFTP or equivalent
  • Accuracy measured against ground truth and reported with the review rate
  • Client data never used to train models for other customers
