How we work with clients

Common questions.

How much does it cost?
It depends on the work, so we don't publish prices. The audit shows you what automation would save before you spend anything on a build. Then we send a written proposal, and there's no obligation to go ahead.
How soon will we see results?
The audit takes one to two weeks. A working test on your own work follows within two to six weeks, so you see real results before committing to a full build.

Your data stays in the UK or EU, or on your own servers if you prefer. Access is controlled and logged, and your data is never used to train AI for anyone else. We'll help with any data protection paperwork you need.
Do we need technical staff?
No. We do the technical work and train your team to use the system. If you'd rather not run it yourselves, we can run it for you.
