Working with us

Most AI consultancies are built to win projects. We're built to keep clients.

It's the business model. Our revenue comes from long-running support, not big builds, so every incentive we have points the same way yours do: systems that work, keep working, and keep paying back.

The relationship

What working with us looks like.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    We learn your business

    From the people who run it, not just the boardroom. You get an honest, scored register of what's worth automating and what isn't.

  2. First months

    A small build

    Success criteria agreed in writing, and you measure us against them. If the numbers aren't there, you walk away with the findings and no hard feelings.

  3. Year one

    Into production

    Your team is trained to challenge the system. When you call, you reach the people who built it, not an account manager.

  4. Years after

    Run, retune, extend

    Your monthly report shows what the system earned, where it's drifting, and what we'd automate next. Most clients add their second and third systems from that list.

Our client charter

The promises that make it work.

Eight commitments, written down so you can hold us to them, and why each one matters to your budget.

01 · Senior-led

The people you meet build your system.

The directors and engineers in your first meeting design, build and support it, and you'll have their direct numbers.

Why it matters: you never pay for senior expertise and get a junior team.

02 · Honest advice

We'll tell you when AI is the wrong answer.

Some processes need a better spreadsheet, not a model. The audit is there to find the truth, not to sell a build.

Why it matters: the cheapest project is the one we talk you out of.

03 · Start small

Prove value before you commit.

Every stage is a small step with a defined outcome and a clean exit. You sign up for the next step, not a transformation programme.

Why it matters: you never spend more than you've seen work.

04 · Clear proposals

Scoped from your requirements.

We understand the problem first, then propose. Scope is agreed in writing before work starts.

Why it matters: no surprises on the invoice.

05 · Your data, your rules

Governance built in, not bolted on.

Data residency, access controls, audit logging and human oversight are designed in from the audit, and documented for your compliance team.

Why it matters: no expensive rework when your DPO or auditor asks questions.

06 · In it for years

A partner, not a project. No lock-in.

Support runs month to month. We'd rather support you for a decade than invoice you once.

Why it matters: we have to re-earn your business every month, which keeps us sharp.

07 · Your team, upskilled

We leave you stronger.

Training and handover are part of every build. Your staff understand the system and can challenge it.

Why it matters: the capability stays with you, not with us.

08 · Reachable

Answers in hours, not ticket queues.

You deal with people who know your system by name. When something needs attention, you call us and it gets fixed.

Why it matters: less downtime means the system keeps paying back.

Straight answers

Success criteria are written before we build. If they're not met at proof of concept, you stop. That's the point of the stage gate.
What does ongoing support buy?
Monitoring, retraining, upgrades, and first call on the team that built your system.
Why work in small steps?
Each stage has its own scope and a clean exit, so you never commit to more than you've already seen work. It also means our success depends on doing each step well.
