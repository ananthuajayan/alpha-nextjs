The A2O process

From Alpha to Omega.

Our name is our method. Every engagement runs through the same four stages. Each is small enough to commit to, and each leaves you with something you keep, whether or not you continue.

No long discovery phasesNo 100-page strategy decksNo lock-in

Stage 1 · Alpha

AI Audit

1–2 weeksYours to keep

We sit with the people who actually do the work, not just the leadership team, and map where hours, errors and cost really accumulate. Then we score each candidate use case on value, feasibility, data readiness and regulatory risk, and tell you plainly which ones are worth pursuing.

If AI isn't the right answer for a process, the audit says so.

  • A prioritised use-case register with value and risk scores
  • A data readiness and governance assessment
  • A recommended first build, scoped and quoted to your requirements

Stage 2 · Beta

Proof of concept

2–6 weeksGo / no-go decision

We build a working system on a slice of your real data and your real workflow, never a canned demo. Your team uses it, breaks it, and tells us what's wrong.

Success criteria are agreed up front, in writing, so there's no ambiguity at the end. We measure accuracy, time saved and error rates against the baseline captured in the audit.

  • A working system your staff have actually used
  • Measured results against the agreed criteria
  • A production plan and proposal to scale it

Stage 3 · Build

Production build

4–12 weeksDelivered in milestonesYour infrastructure or ours

The proof of concept becomes a hardened system: security review, access controls, audit logging, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints where regulation or good sense demands them. It integrates with the systems you already run.

A dedicated test analyst tests everything before it touches live operations, and we train the staff who will own it.

  • A deployed system with monitoring, logging and rollback
  • A governance pack: data flows, model documentation and audit trail
  • Staff training and handover documentation

Stage 4 · Omega

Run & improve

Monthly supportCancel with notice

AI systems drift. Documents change format, call patterns shift, models are updated. We monitor accuracy in production, retrain and tune as your data changes, and extend the system to new use cases from your audit register.

We act as your on-call AI team. Most clients stay in this stage for years, and it's where the relationship pays off for both of us.

  • Monthly accuracy and performance reporting
  • Continuous tuning, retraining and model upgrades
  • A standing roadmap of next automations

Why the process looks like this

Each stage answers a reason AI projects fail.

Alpha answers

"We haven't found a clear use for AI."

71% of UK non-adopters give this as their reason. The audit starts from where your hours actually go, so the first build targets a real, measured problem.

Beta answers

"Nobody agreed what success looked like."

Most generative AI pilots never show measurable impact. Written success criteria, measured against a baseline, make the go / no-go decision a matter of evidence.

Build answers

"Our team couldn't do it alone."

Pilots that pair internal teams with external specialists succeed about 67% of the time, against about 22% for internal-only builds. We build with your people, not around them.

Omega answers

"It worked at launch, then got worse."

Models drift as data changes. Ongoing monitoring and retraining keep accuracy where it was on day one, and catch problems before your users do.

Sources: MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025; UK Government DSIT AI Adoption Research and ONS. Figures are paraphrased.
