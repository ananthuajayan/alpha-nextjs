What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Some are needed for a site to work. Others track what you do, often for advertising.

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It does not currently set any cookies at all, and it doesn't store preferences in your browser. Light and dark mode follow your device setting.

Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings › Privacy › Block all cookies
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings › Cookies and site permissions

If this changes

If we ever add a tool that needs consent, such as Google Analytics, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent before it sets any cookies, as the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) require.
