Client context

Orthopic is building decision support for orthodontic referrals. It is being validated with an NHS orthodontic practice in North East England.

The manual process and its cost

Every orthodontic referral needs a face-to-face assessment appointment, including many that clearly qualify for NHS treatment on referral photographs alone. Those appointments use clinic capacity and lengthen waiting lists.

What we built

  • A guided patient photo-capture web app: six standardised poses on a phone, with automatic quality checking
  • An AI analysis pipeline that assesses traits relevant to IOTN, the Index of Orthodontic Treatment Need
  • A clinician review station where a specialist confirms every outcome
  • A triage policy that only routes automatically where independent AI signals agree. Everything else goes to human review by design.

Each referral is routed to the right queue: likely NHS qualifier, face-to-face assessment, or priority review. Orthopic is decision support with clinician sign-off. It does not diagnose.

How we tested it

Orthopic is being tested in clinical validation with an NHS orthodontic practice. Real referral sessions are captured and labelled by specialists, so the system is judged against clinical opinion on real patients. A clinician reviews every outcome throughout.

Results

Hundreds of real referral sessions have been captured and specialist-labelled. The system decides only where it is reliable and defers the rest to clinicians. The regulatory pathway (UKCA, DCB0129, DTAC, DSPT, DPIA) is in progress.

What changed operationally

During validation, patients take their own referral photos on a phone, guided step by step, and a specialist reviews each case at the review station. The aim is to cut unnecessary face-to-face assessments for referrals that clearly qualify, and to shorten waiting lists by triaging at the point of referral.

The design is meant to earn clinical trust: clinician sign-off on everything, and honesty about what it can't yet decide.

Where the relationship went next

The regulatory pathway (UKCA, DCB0129, DTAC, DSPT, DPIA) is in progress alongside clinical validation.
