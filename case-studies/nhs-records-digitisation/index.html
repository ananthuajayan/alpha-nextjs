Client context

The client is a UK records storage company digitising NHS medical records. NHS trusts rely on it for fast, accurate access to patient records once they are digitised.

The manual process and its cost

Manual data entry was both the bottleneck and the main cost. Keeping up with trust requests meant hiring temporary data-entry staff.

What we built

A full DocBox deployment on UK-resident infrastructure in the London region. We deployed it under our Rapid Deploy model, configured to the client's records and output formats.

The governance work ran alongside the software. As part of delivery, we completed the NHS trusts' ICT security questionnaires.

The accuracy trial

When we run an accuracy trial, we measure every field against verified answers on the client's own documents, not a test set. [Trial method and figures for this deployment: Bijesh to confirm]
