Case study · Maritime & logistics

Tracking every pallet, even offline.

An offline-first app built for warehouse conditions, where connectivity drops and nothing can go unrecorded.

Client
A records storage and logistics operator, Northern Ireland
Sector
Maritime & logistics
Status
Phase 1 shipped and in use

The challenge

A records storage operator needed to know where every pallet was across its sites, in buildings where mobile and Wi-Fi signal can't be relied on.

What we built

  • An offline-first progressive web app for pallet tracking across sites: scan, move and locate
  • Designed for warehouse conditions, syncing when connectivity returns
  • Phase 2 scoped: CCTV computer-vision tracking to capture movements automatically

How we delivered it

Phase 1 put a working tool in staff hands first. Phase 2 adds CCTV computer-vision tracking.

Results

Phase 1 has shipped and is in use.

What the client valued

A tool built for the warehouse floor as it is, not as a demo imagines it.

Where the relationship went next
