The challenge

A busy helpdesk was answering the same questions repeatedly. The answers existed, but they were buried in a knowledge base and years of resolved tickets.

What we built

  • An analysis of around 17,500 historical support tickets
  • A retrieval pipeline over the knowledge base and ticket history
  • Deployment inside the client's existing Zoho Support environment, with real-time sync
  • Agent review and editing, response rating, and an add-to-knowledge-base loop

How we delivered it

  1. Alpha: analysis of the ticket history to find what was routine and answerable.
  2. Beta and Build: a retrieval pipeline deployed inside the helpdesk the team already used.
  3. Omega: ratings and the knowledge-base loop improve coverage over time.

Return on investment

The cost of the problem
Seven agents on a queue answering the same questions repeatedly.
Measured return
Queue staffing down to five, with two experienced people freed for complex tickets.

Results

Support headcount on the queue went from seven agents to five, with the AI drafting first responses for agent review.

What the client valued

  • It worked inside the helpdesk they already had
  • Agents stayed in control of every reply
