An NRM2 Bill of Quantities from drawings.

BQai on a live residential development tender in Belfast.

Project
A residential development tender, Belfast
Sector
Construction
Product
BQai

The challenge

Producing a Bill of Quantities by hand is one of the slowest jobs in pre-construction. Measuring from drawings can take weeks before pricing even starts.

What we built

Using BQai, we produced a full NRM2-compliant Bill of Quantities for a live residential scheme, working from the architect's drawing set.

Results

The project demonstrated drawing-to-BoQ automation on real tender documents, not a test set.

What it shows

BQai works on live tender drawings and produces output in the NRM2 structure quantity surveyors already use.
