The problem

Every referral needs an appointment. Many don't need to.

Every orthodontic referral gets a face-to-face assessment, including the many that clearly qualify for NHS treatment from their photographs alone. Those appointments take clinic time and make waiting lists longer for everyone.

Orthopic helps you see which referrals clearly qualify at the point of referral, so assessment time goes to the patients who need it.

How it works

Four steps, with a clinician at the end of each one.

  1. Guided capture

    Patients take six standardised photos on their phone. The app checks quality as they go and asks for a retake if a photo is too dark or blurred. It has been used in more than 700 patient capture sessions.

  2. Analysis

    The system assesses the traits relevant to IOTN, the Index of Orthodontic Treatment Need.

  3. Abstain when unsure

    It only suggests a route when independent signals agree. Anything else goes straight to human review. It decides only where it's reliable.

  4. Clinician review

    A specialist confirms every outcome on the review station: likely NHS qualifier, face-to-face assessment, or priority review.

The review station

The clinician always has the final say.

The station shows the referral's photos and the suggested queue. Nothing is routed until a clinician confirms or changes it.

Straight talk

What Orthopic is, and what it isn't.

It is

  • Decision support for triaging referrals
  • Hundreds of real referral sessions, captured and labelled by specialists
  • Designed to defer to a clinician whenever it isn't sure

It isn't

  • A diagnosis. A clinician signs off every outcome.
  • A finished, certified product yet. Validation and the regulatory pathway are in progress.
  • A replacement for clinical judgement.
