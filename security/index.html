Trust

Security & data protection.

How we protect your data and keep people in control of the systems we build. Written for compliance teams, DPOs and IT security reviewers.

Data residency

Your data stays where you agree.

Systems are deployed on UK-resident cloud infrastructure (London region), or in the EU (Dublin) for Irish clients, agreed per engagement. On-premises deployment is available where required.

Governance by design

Built in, not bolted on.

Every production build ships with access controls, audit logging, human-in-the-loop checkpoints where regulation or risk demands them, and a governance pack (data flows, model documentation, audit trail) written for compliance teams.

Healthcare

Experienced with NHS assurance.

We have experience working through DTAC, DCB0129, DSPT, DPIAs and trust ICT security questionnaires.

Model & data commitments

Your data serves you only.

Client data is never used to train models for other customers. Retrieval and processing are scoped to approved document sets, and document pipelines use SFTP or other encrypted channels.

Responsible AI

Honest about what AI can and can't decide.

  • A person reviews consequential outputs before they take effect
  • Abstain-when-uncertain policies: systems defer to people rather than guess
  • Accuracy measured against ground truth and reported honestly, including review rates
