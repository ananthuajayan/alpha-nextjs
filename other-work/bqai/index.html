BQai

Bills of Quantities, from weeks to days.

BQai takes architectural drawings and produces NRM2-compliant Bills of Quantities, automating one of pre-construction's most laborious document workflows.

Who it's for

Quantity surveyors, contractors and estimators.

Anyone who prices work from drawings and spends days measuring before they can start pricing.

How it works

Drawings in, BoQ out.

  1. Upload drawings

    Provide the architectural drawing set for the scheme.

  2. Measure

    BQai identifies and measures the elements in the drawings.

  3. Structure

    Quantities are organised into an NRM2-compliant Bill of Quantities.

  4. Review

    Your QS checks, adjusts and approves the draft before it's used.
