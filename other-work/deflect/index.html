Who it's for

If your team answers the same questions every day and the answers already exist somewhere, this puts them in front of your agents in seconds.

How it works

Inside the helpdesk you already have.

  1. Sync

    Tickets sync in real time from your existing helpdesk, such as Zoho Support.

  2. Retrieve

    The system finds relevant answers in your knowledge base and resolved tickets, scoped to document sets you approve.

  3. Draft

    A reply is drafted in British English, under the right department's sender identity.

  4. Review and rate

    An agent edits, approves and rates the draft before it's sent.

  5. Improve

    Good answers can be added to the knowledge base in one step, so coverage grows over time.
