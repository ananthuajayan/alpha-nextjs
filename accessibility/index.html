Our aim

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. You should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen
  • navigate the whole site using a keyboard, starting with a "Skip to content" link
  • use the site with a screen reader
  • use light or dark mode, following your device setting
  • turn off animation by switching on "reduce motion" in your device settings
