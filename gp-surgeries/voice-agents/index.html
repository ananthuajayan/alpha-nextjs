How it works

Answer, triage, route, record.

  1. Answer

    Every call is picked up, day or night, in natural language.

  2. Triage

    A structured script establishes what the caller needs and how urgent it is.

  3. Route or escalate

    Requests go to the right team, and anything outside the script goes straight to a person.

  4. Record

    A call summary is filed to your line-of-business system.

Governance
