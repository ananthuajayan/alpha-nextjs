Two things we do for practices

Answer every call. Sort every letter.

1 · Patient-line voice agent

Every call answered, even at 8am.

The agent picks up every call and works out what the patient needs using a structured, five-tier urgency framework. Routine requests go to the right team with a summary already filed to the patient record. Anything urgent or unclear goes straight to a member of staff.

  • Works with your practice clinical system
  • A clear path to a person on every call
  • Every call logged

2 · Clinical letter classifier

Letters sorted before anyone opens them.

Incoming letters are read, coded with SNOMED CT and matched to the right workflow queue. Your staff see a suggestion for each one and approve or change it. Nothing reaches a patient record until a person has signed it off.

  • Suggestions follow how your experienced staff already file
  • Straightforward letters first, then the trickier ones
  • Accuracy improves through supervised use

A morning at reception

Before and after.

Before

  • 8am: every line is busy and patients are stuck in a queue.
  • Reception juggles the phones and the front desk at the same time.
  • Letters pile up in the tray until someone has time to read them.
  • Afternoons go on reading, coding and filing letters by hand.

After

  • 8am: every call is answered and routine requests are routed with a summary.
  • Reception handles the urgent and the complicated, in person or on the phone.
  • Letters arrive sorted, with a suggested code and workflow for each.
  • Staff approve letters in batches instead of starting from scratch.
